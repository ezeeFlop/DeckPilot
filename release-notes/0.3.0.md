## Two-state keys

A key can now have a second look and a second action. Under **Second state** in a key's
settings, choose what switches it:

- every press, for a plain toggle;
- the Mac's sound being muted;
- an application being open;
- a command succeeding (checked every 5 seconds).

Then pick **State 2** at the top of the panel and set its title, icon, background and
action with the same controls as the first state.
