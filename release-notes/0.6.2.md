## Fixes

- The deck stopped refreshing after a few hours when a key was left selected in the settings
  window, most visibly on pages with plugin keys. On macOS 26, a segmented picker loses a
  little memory every time it is redrawn; the settings panel was redrawn on every plugin
  update, so the window's layout grew slower and slower until it took all of the app's time.
  The two segmented pickers of the panel are now redrawn only when their choice changes.
- Copying a plugin key and pasting it on the same page closed the app. The pasted key now
  gets its own identity for the plugin, and keeps the settings of the original.
