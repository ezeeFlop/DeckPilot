## Folders

Right-click a key and choose **Create a folder here**: the key now opens a page of its own,
which already has its **Back** key. Folders stay out of the next/previous page cycle. Any
page can be turned into a folder, or back into an ordinary page, from its menu in the sidebar.

## Pages that follow your apps

Right-click a page in the sidebar and choose **Linked applications…**. When one of them
comes to the front, the deck shows that page; when you leave, it returns to the page it
was on.
