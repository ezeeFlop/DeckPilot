## Live data on your keys

A key can now show a number that keeps itself up to date:

- a Mac measurement: processor, memory, disk, battery;
- a field read from a JSON API;
- the output of a shell command.

Draw it as a value, a ring, a bar or a sparkline, set the range and the unit, and choose
the thresholds where it turns orange and red. The gauge glides from one reading to the next.

Pick it under **Live content** in a key's settings. It works on the three display-only
cells of the strip too.
